# CivicConnect – A Collaborative Civic Problem-Solving and Community Development Platform

## Project Overview

CivicConnect is a web-based platform that connects citizens, students, educational institutions, industries, and government authorities to identify and solve real-world public problems.

It is not just a complaint registration portal. It creates a complete ecosystem where citizens report public issues, students propose innovative solutions, industries provide mentorship and resources, and government authorities verify, approve, and coordinate implementation.

## Main Objective

To bridge the gap between public problems and practical solutions through collaboration, innovation, transparency, and citizen participation.

## Stakeholders and Their Roles

### 1. Citizens
- Register and log in.
- Report public problems with descriptions, photographs, categories, severity, and location.
- Track their reported problems.
- View verification and implementation updates.
- Provide feedback.

### 2. Students and Colleges
- Browse verified public problems.
- Select problems and submit innovative proposals.
- Upload project documents, prototypes, and demonstrations.
- Form student teams.
- Collaborate with industries and government authorities.

### 3. Industries
- Explore public problems and student proposals.
- Provide mentorship and technical expertise.
- Offer funding, equipment, and other resources.
- Collaborate with educational institutions.
- Discover innovative ideas and student talent.

### 4. Government Authorities
- View and verify reported problems.
- Reject false or duplicate reports.
- Set problem priorities.
- Assign relevant departments.
- Publish verified problems.
- Review and approve proposed solutions.
- Coordinate implementation and monitor progress.

## Core Workflow

Citizen Reporting → Government Verification → Problem Publication → Student Solution Proposals → Industry Support → Government Approval → Implementation → Progress Tracking → Citizen Feedback.

## Main Features

1. User registration and role-based login.
2. Citizen problem reporting with photo uploads.
3. Interactive map-based location selection.
4. Unique problem identification numbers.
5. Personal dashboard and report tracking.
6. Government verification and management queue.
7. Problem categorisation and priority assignment.
8. Student proposal submission.
9. Industry mentorship and support offers.
10. Notifications and status history.
11. Interactive dashboards and progress reports.

## Technology Stack

- Frontend: React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS.
- Backend services: Supabase.
- Database: PostgreSQL through Supabase.
- Authentication: Supabase Auth.
- Maps: Leaflet and OpenStreetMap.
- Future AI features: Python and machine-learning libraries.
- Version control: Git and GitHub.

## Planned AI Enhancements

- Automatic problem categorisation.
- Duplicate problem detection.
- Priority recommendations.
- Matching student teams with relevant problems.
- Matching industries with suitable proposals.
- Analysing public feedback.

## Expected Impact

**Citizens:** Easier problem reporting and transparent tracking.

**Students:** Real-world project experience and opportunities to apply technical knowledge.

**Industries:** Access to innovative solutions and emerging talent.

**Government:** Better problem management, collaboration, and implementation coordination.

**Society:** Improved public services, innovation, accountability, and community development.

## Long-Term Vision

To create a unified digital ecosystem where public problems become opportunities for innovation and collaboration, contributing to smarter communities and national development.
